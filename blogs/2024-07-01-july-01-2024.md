v1 Announcement As of July 1, 2024, API gateways located in asia-east1 are decommissioned and will no longer serve traffic. Between October 2021 and October 2022, customers with gateways located in asia-east1 were notified of the planned decommissioning and advised to delete or relocate any gateways in this region. A final reminder was sent in May, 2024. As of July 1, 2024, any remaining gateways located in asia-east1 are fully decommissioned.
